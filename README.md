## Overview

Imagenomic Portraiture is an industry‑standard plugin for portrait and beauty retouching. It leverages proprietary artificial intelligence and computer vision algorithms to automatically detect skin regions with sub‑pixel accuracy, applying smoothing and texture refinement exclusively where needed — preserving critical details such as pores, eyebrows, eyelashes, and hair strands.

The 2026 release introduces enhanced neural network models for improved skin‑tone separation, faster GPU‑accelerated processing, and refined threshold controls that adapt to varying image resolutions and lighting conditions. Portraiture is trusted by professional photographers, retouchers, and studios worldwide for its consistency, speed, and output quality.

## Key Features

- **Intelligent Auto‑Masking** – AI‑driven skin detection works across diverse ethnicities, ages, and lighting scenarios with zero manual masking.
- **Adaptive Smoothing** – Applies variable‑strength smoothing based on local detail frequency: fine pores get minimal smoothing, while larger blemishes receive stronger correction.
- **Preserve Texture** – Unique algorithm separates skin texture from tone, ensuring natural‑looking results without the “plastic” effect.
- **Threshold & Detail Controls** – Granular sliders for fine, medium, and coarse detail allow precise artistic control.
- **GPU Acceleration** – Utilises OpenCL and Metal for near‑real‑time preview and batch processing.
- **Batch Processing** – Apply consistent retouching to hundreds of images via Photoshop Actions or Lightroom batch export.
- **Custom Presets** – Save and share your favourite settings for different genres (beauty, wedding, editorial, etc.).
- **Non‑Destructive Workflow** – Operates as a filter layer, preserving the original image data.

## First‑Time Usage (Quick Start)

1. Open any portrait or group photo in Photoshop.
2. Apply the Portraiture filter from the menu.
3. In the plugin interface, you will see a real‑time preview.
4. Adjust the **Smoothing** slider to control overall skin effect.
5. Use the **Threshold** slider to define which details are smoothed — higher threshold includes more areas.
6. Switch to **Mask View** (toggle) to confirm that only skin areas are selected.
7. Click **OK** to apply and return to your host application.
